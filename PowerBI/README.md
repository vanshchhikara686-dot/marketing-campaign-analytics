# Power BI Dashboard – Marketing Campaign Analytics

## Overview

This folder contains Power BI dashboard screenshots developed for the Marketing Campaign Performance & ROI Analytics project.

## Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard explores:

* Total marketing spend
* Attributed revenue
* Total conversions
* Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)
* Revenue by marketing channel
* ROAS by marketing channel
* Revenue by campaign
* ROAS by campaign
* Monthly revenue trends

## Interactive Analysis

The dashboard includes filters for campaign channels, campaign types, and reporting periods.

## Business Objective

To help marketing teams understand campaign effectiveness, compare channel performance, and make better budget allocation decisions.

## Technology

Microsoft Power BI
